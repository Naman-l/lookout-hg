- A **PO line** is `<po number>/<line>`. Ordered, promised and received are three different dates. **Open** means open or partly received; **past due** means open and promised before the data date (a line promised on the data date is not yet past due).
- **Money**: the ERP's price and currency are kept raw; the dollar price is converted at finance's rate for the order month; a blank currency is filled from the supplier and labelled inferred. A price in thousands, by the box or by the foot is converted to a price each, or left unknown when the pack size is not in the data.
- **Flags on a line**: `return`; `short_closed` (closed with less than ordered); `stale_open` (ordered over a year ago and still open: probably closed in reality, so left out of supply and raised as a question); `no_promise_date` (it can never be late by date, so it needs chasing); `received_before_ordered` (the received date precedes the order: shown as unknown, never as a delivery); `price_outlier` and `decimal_slip` (far from the part's history: kept, never used as a price); `box_size_unknown`; `non_active_supplier`; `legacy_id_in_new_po`; `dup_removed`.
- A part's **reference price** is its latest clean line. With none, the demand for it is unknown in money.
- A supplier's **revised date** replaces the promised date only when it was announced before the line fell due and is still ahead.
