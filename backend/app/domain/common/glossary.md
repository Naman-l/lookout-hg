- **Every number says where it came from.** A money amount or a quantity carries its provenance: `stated` (read from a record), `extracted` (read from a line of a message or a document), `computed` (code worked it out; the records it used are named), `inferred` (a rule filled it in, and says so), `flagged` (kept but doubted) or `unknown`. Quote the provenance, never improve on it.
- **Unknown is a value, not a blank.** It names what is missing, why that blocks the answer, who can answer, and the question to ask. Never turn it into zero or a guess, and never say "none" for it.
- **Raw beside clean.** Where the build changed a value, the source value is kept beside it, so any figure can be traced to its row or its line.
- **The data date is the clock.** "Past due" and "days left" count from the data date, and nothing dated after it exists yet; never use the wall clock.
